Strategies

What remedik is allowed to do, and for which alerts. Strategies are cluster-scoped resources you keep in git; this page only reads them.

Matchers are equality only, and the most specific matching strategy wins. Both guards are opt-in: a strategy that sets neither may run as often as its alert fires.
